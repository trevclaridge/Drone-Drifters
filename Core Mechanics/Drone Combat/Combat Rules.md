# Combat Rules

## Combat Start

Combat is divided into **Rounds**. Each round, each Drifter may take a **Turn**. Between each Drifter turn, the GM will take a turn for the remaining enemy combatants. Once each combatant has had a turn in a round, a new round begins.

At the beginning of each round, all Drifters roll their **Drift Dice standard pool**. For most Drifters this will mean rolling 4 d6s, however some Drifters may have effects that alter their standard pool.

In order to determine which side between the Drifters and enemies goes first, the Drifters will roll a group **Readiness** Test. Drifters each roll a d6 and add their results together. If the Drifters' summed Readiness roll value is greater than the summed Readiness scores of their enemies, the Drifters get the drop and go first; if the summed value is less than the enemies' Readiness, the enemies detect your arrival and they go first. Drifters can add an additional point of Readiness to their score for each Cohesion they are willing to spend.

## On A Turn

### Systems and Activation Slots

On each turn, a Drifter spends their Drift Dice to perform actions via the drone(s) they pilot. Each system on a drone will list a **Cost** value; to activate that system, a Drifter must assign a number of Drift Dice to that system where the pips on the dice equal or exceed the Cost value. Those dice are now spent, and cannot be used to activate any other systems. Additionally, those dice go toward filling up that system's **Activation Slots**; each system has a number of Activation Slots which represent the number of individual Drift Dice that it can sustain each round (usually 4). When a system's Activation Slots are full, that system is overheated and cannot be used until it's Activation Slots are cleared. Drifters reclaim all spent Drift Dice when they go to reroll their standard pool at the beginning of a new round, clearing Activation Slots in the process. Cohesion can also be used to clear Activation Slots as needed at any tine.

Activation Slots also act as a systems final durability. As a system takes damage, its durability is reduced. Most systems will have some buffer durability that is reduced first, but once all that buffer is destroyed, a system's Activation Slots are destroyed instead. For each point of damage takes beyond that system's buffer durability, an activation slot is removed.

### Move

On their turn, Drifters can also **Move**. Drifters get one free movement per turn, allowing them to move a drone they are piloting up that drone's Speed. The section on Speed in the Drone Statistics has more information on how to pilot (pg. xx). Drifters can gain additional Move actions by spending Drift Dice--Drifters can take an additional Move action for each single Drift Die allocated to an open Activation Slot anywhere on the drone they are currently piloting.

## Drift

Finally, each Drifter also has a free **Drift** action they an take at the end of each of their turns. This is the thing that separates Drifters from everyone else: the mental fortitude to survive flinging their consciousnesses across the thought plane and still being able to *think* after. Drifters can take an additional Drift action at any point during their turn for each single Drift Die they are willing to spend towards that end. Drift Dice spent this way are not allocated to an Activation Slot and instead are sacrificed until they are reclaimed at the beginning of a new round.

Whenever a Drifter Drifts into a new drone, any Drifters currently piloting that drone can choose to **Bounce**. Bouncing is Drifting to a new drone without using their free Drift per turn or sacrificing any Drift Dice.

## Systems

## Front

Front systems shoot out the front, as simple as that really. Systems marked with the **Front** keyword must be installed in the Front system area of a drone. Weapon systems installed on the Front shoot in a straight line out out of the front of the drone and strike the first target within the system's **Range**.
